Privacy

Nothing is collected. The web version of Flex has no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies, and it never sends your workouts anywhere.

Where your data lives

  • Your workouts, history and settings are stored only in this browser on this device.
  • They are not backed up or synced. Clearing this website's data in your browser settings, or deleting the Home Screen app, deletes them.

"Use your own AI"

You copy a prompt into your own AI app (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…) yourself, and paste its answer back. Flex reads that answer on your device. Flex never contacts any AI service; what you share with your AI app is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is served by a web host (Vercel). Like any website, the host processes standard technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages. Flex itself adds nothing to that.

Add Flex to your Home Screen

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, then Add. Other browsers: open the browser menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

During a workout, keep Flex open with the screen on: locking the phone or switching apps pauses the sounds. The timer itself stays correct and catches up when you come back.
